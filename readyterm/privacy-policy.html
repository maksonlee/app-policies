Termstay Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Termstay for Android is developed by Makson Lee. This Privacy Policy explains how Termstay for Android handles user data.

1. Core functionality

Termstay for Android connects your phone to your own Termstay server: the Termstay server on Ubuntu, or Termstay on a Windows PC with Sharing turned on. It starts SSH sessions on that server from the connections you save, lists the sessions running there, shows their output, and lets you type into a session you take control of.

2. Direct connection to your own server

The app connects directly to the servers you add. It does not use developer-controlled servers, accounts, cloud services, or relays. Session output and what you type travel only between your phone, your server and the SSH host you connect to. The password or key a session signs in with goes only to your server, which uses it to sign in to that SSH host.

The app handles only:

  • the IP address and port of each server you add
  • each server's identity, and the access that server grants your phone
  • the connections you save: their names, hosts, ports and usernames
  • the passwords you choose to have it remember, and the private keys you give it
  • the output of the sessions you open, and what you type into them

3. Data collection

Termstay for Android does not require user accounts and does not collect personal information such as name, email address, phone number, location, or payment information.

Termstay for Android contains no advertising and does not collect analytics. The developer receives none of the data the app handles.

4. Permissions and device access

Termstay for Android requests only network access, so the app can reach the servers you add. It does not keep a connection open in the background: when you return to the app, it connects to your servers again.

5. Data sharing

Termstay for Android does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties.

The app may rely on standard Android and Google Play platform components to run on the device, but it does not transmit session output, what you type, passwords, keys, or usage data to developer-operated services.

6. Data retention and deletion

The servers you add, with their identities and access grants, and the connections, passwords and private keys you save stay in the app's private storage on your device until you remove them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

Terminal output is kept only while a session is open in the app. The developer retains no user data.

7. Security

The connection to your server is encrypted, and the app checks the server's identity each time it connects. A server must approve your phone before the phone can see, start or control any of its sessions, and the server can revoke that access at any time.

Passwords and private keys are stored encrypted with a key held in Android's keystore. They are sent only to your own server, over that encrypted connection, when a session signs in; the server uses them for that sign-in and does not store them.

8. Children

Termstay for Android is intended for adults and is not directed to children.
